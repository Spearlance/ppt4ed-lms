{# Remembers where a visitor came from so signups and enrollments can be attributed in Admin Reports > Sources. Included by the /c/, /r/ and /e/ landing pages and by the Vue app shell (frontend/index.html). Read server-side by lms/lms/traffic_source.py. Keep the cookie name and the source/medium/campaign/referrer keys in sync with that module. A tagged link (utm_source, gclid, fbclid) always replaces the stored source. A bare referrer only fills an empty slot, so coming back through a search engine does not erase the campaign that first brought the visitor in. No cookie at all means a direct visit. #}
